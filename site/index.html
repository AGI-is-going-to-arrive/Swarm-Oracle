换一个选择，
会发生什么？What changes when you choose differently?

写下一个“如果……”。让 AI 角色从各自的立场出发，演出几种走向。你可以读结局、追问角色，再改一个选择，看故事从哪里开始不同。

Ask “what if?” and let AI characters play out different paths. Read the endings, question the characters, then change a choice and see where the story diverges.

从样例认识它Explore a sample 在本地打开Run it locally

这是介绍页。请先在自己的电脑上运行应用，再打开样例。This is the introduction site. Run the app on your computer to open a sample.

先打开一个已经完成的样例Start with a finished sample

先看看结果长什么样，再决定要不要配置模型。

See what a result looks like before setting up a model.

  1. 在首页打开官方样例Open an official sample from Home

    应用自带三套样例。打开样例不需要模型密钥，也不会调用模型。

    The app includes three samples. Opening one needs no model key and makes no model call.

  2. 先读摘要，再选一条结局Read the summary, then choose an ending

    每条“世界线”就是一种故事走向。比较角色的选择和结果，不必先读完所有图表。

    A “worldline” is one possible story. Compare its choices and ending without reading every chart first.

  3. 选一个问题继续看Pick one question to follow

    想知道依据，就查发言和行动；想试别的走法，就改写。生成新内容时需要可用的模型配置。

    Check messages and actions for the basis of a claim, or rewrite a choice to try another path. Generating new content needs a working model connection.

读完结局，还可以继续What to do after reading a result

查依据，也可以追问Check the basis. Ask a follow-up.

从报告回到对应的轮次、发言和行动，看看角色为什么这样判断。也可以点图上的节点，先看详情，再追问这个节点。想一起讨论某个结局，就进入结局会客厅；有几条结局时，可以让不同世界线的代表坐下来讨论。

Follow a report back to its rounds, messages, and actions. You can also select a graph node, read its details, and ask about that node. Discuss an ending in an Ending Chamber, or bring representatives of different endings together at a Worldline Roundtable.

报告里的依据来自这次推演。请另外核实现实中的事实。Report evidence comes from this simulation. Verify real-world facts separately.

改一句话，比较两条走向Rewrite a line. Compare the paths.

选中一条发言，写下另一种说法，再生成后续。对照原来的分支和改写后的分支，看谁改变了行动、差异从哪一轮开始。

Choose a message, rewrite it, and generate what follows. Compare the original and rewritten branches to see whose actions changed and where the paths began to differ.

一次改写展示的是模型在这些设定下的结果，不证明现实里一定会这样。A rewrite shows what the model produced under these assumptions. It does not establish what would happen in reality.

读结果时，分清三件事Read proposals, plans, and actions separately

提出建议 Proposed

角色说了想怎么做。

A character says what they want to do.

采用建议 Adopted

这个做法进入了计划，还需要查看执行状态。

The idea is part of the plan. Check whether it was carried out.

实际执行 Executed

行动记录确认它在这次推演中发生。可以继续查看目标、结果和前后变化。

The action record confirms it happened in this simulation. Inspect its target, outcome, and before-and-after state.

图上的权重和报告中的置信提示，只描述这次推演的结果或依据。不要把它们读成现实事件发生的概率。

Weights and confidence indicators describe this run and its available evidence. They are not probabilities of real-world events.

只想把争议说清楚？试试辩论For a focused disagreement, try a debate

正方、反方与一位评委按固定流程讨论同一命题。看完裁决，再查看发言和论点图，判断哪些理由站得住。

Two sides and one judge discuss a proposition in a fixed format. After the verdict, review the speeches and argument map to assess the reasons.

  1. 开场 Opening
  2. 交锋 Crossfire
  3. 反驳 Rebuttal
  4. 结辩 Closing
  5. 裁决 Verdict

首页的推演轮数和角色数量不会改变这套辩论结构。评委的裁决是本次讨论的评价，仍需你核对事实和理由。Simulation round and character settings do not change this debate format. The judge evaluates the discussion; you still need to check its facts and reasoning.

在自己的电脑上打开Run it on your computer

先运行应用、打开样例。准备好自己的问题后，再连接模型。

Run the app and open a sample first. Connect a model when you are ready to ask your own question.

用 Docker 启动Start with Docker

安装 Git 和 Docker，并启动 Docker 后，在一个新目录里运行以下命令。Install Git and Docker, start Docker, then run these commands in a new directory.

Terminal
git clone https://github.com/AGI-is-going-to-arrive/Swarm-Oracle.git
cd Swarm-Oracle
docker compose up --build -d

启动完成后打开 http://127.0.0.1:18928Open this local address after startup.

默认只允许本机访问。Docker 需要下载并构建依赖，首次启动时间取决于网络和设备。The default setup is accessible only from your computer. Docker downloads and builds dependencies; first startup depends on your network and hardware.

部署与备份 Deployment & backup本地开发步骤 Local development

生成新内容前，先连接模型Connect a model for new content

在应用的设置页填写服务地址、模型名称和所需密钥，并测试连接。受支持的本地服务可以不填密钥；远程服务需要密钥。

In the app’s setup page, enter the service URL, model name, and required key, then test the connection. Supported local services can work without a key; remote services require one.

连接测试、新推演、追问、改写、辩论和报告可能产生模型费用。等待时间和费用取决于模型、轮数、分支及重试；搜索服务另行配置。Connection tests, new runs, follow-ups, rewrites, debates, and reports may incur model charges. Time and cost depend on the model, rounds, branches, and retries. Search needs separate setup.

查看模型配置说明 Read the model setup guide

本地保存，自己决定分享什么Keep local records. Choose what to share.

自托管时，你管理自己的记录。使用远程模型或搜索服务时，相关问题、材料和上下文会发送到所选服务；“本地运行”不代表这些请求离线。

When you self-host, you manage your records. Remote models and search services receive the relevant questions, materials, and context. Running the app locally does not make those requests offline.

分享时可导出脱敏文件，再由离线画廊打开。导出前仍要检查问题、结局和自填文本中的隐私。导出文件不会替你发布到在线社区。

Export a redacted file to share it through the offline Gallery. Review questions, endings, and custom text for private details before sharing. Exporting a file does not publish it to an online community.
