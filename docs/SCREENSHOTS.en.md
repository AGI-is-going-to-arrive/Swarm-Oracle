# Screenshot tour / 真实界面导览

Find a screen by the task you want to complete. Each group shows this page's language first; expand it for the other language. Open an image to inspect the original.

按你要完成的事情找界面。每组先展示本文语言，展开后可看另一种语言；点击图片可查看原图。

These screenshots show the real application on 2026-09-19. The island city of Harborlight, its battery-allocation question, characters, and background material are fictional. AI conceptual illustrations elsewhere in the documentation have separate labels.

截图拍摄于 2026-09-19，展示当日版本的真实应用界面。灯港群岛的电池分配问题、人物和背景材料均为虚构示例。文档中的 AI 概念插画另有标注。

The Chinese and English screenshots come from separate runs, so characters, statements, and endings may differ.

中英文截图来自分别运行的推演，因此角色、发言和结局可能不同。

Weights, analysis, conversations, and verdicts describe this fictional simulation. Check the status and available actions in your own run as you follow along. See the [usage guide](USAGE.en.md) for the full steps.

模拟权重、分析、对话和裁决只描述这次虚构模拟。跟着操作时，请核对自己页面的完成状态和可用按钮；完整步骤见[使用指南](USAGE.md)。
